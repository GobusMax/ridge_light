How Ridge Light works

The planner estimates when you reach each part of your GPX route. It then traces a line toward the sun through a digital elevation model. If terrain rises above that line, the route is in terrain shade.

  • Base speed is constant. Optional climb time adds 6 seconds/metre for hiking or custom, 10 for ski touring, and 3.6 for running. Breaks, footing and downhill speed changes are not modelled.
  • Times use the trailhead’s time zone. GPX timestamps are ignored. If a clock time occurs twice when clocks go back, the earlier one is used.
  • Terrain is checked out to 45 km. Buildings, trees, clouds, snow and atmospheric refraction are not modelled. “Below horizon” means the sun’s centre is below the astronomical horizon; twilight can still be bright.
  • Your GPX is read in your browser. For imported routes, the terrain service receives requests for the surrounding tile coordinates, not the GPX file.
